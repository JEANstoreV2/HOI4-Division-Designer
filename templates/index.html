Division Stats

Saved Divisions

Save the current division (every section - Composition, Research, Special Projects, Doctrine, Tank Designer designs, Terrain) under a name, then load it back later or compare two side by side.

Research

Check off completed technologies. Research is permanent — once checked, a technology can't be unchecked. Composition only offers battalions/support companies/regimental support once the technology that unlocks them is done.

Special Projects

Check off completed Special Projects. Like Research, this is permanent and separate from it — some technologies and Tank Designer content require a Special Project completed first, in addition to their own research.

Grand Doctrine

Pick a Grand Doctrine per folder, assign a Subdoctrine to each of its tracks, then claim each subdoctrine's rewards in order below. Mastering the right track unlocks a 5th battalion row in Composition; doctrine bonuses feed into Division Stats automatically.

AI Design Comparison

Generates several candidate divisions from whatever's currently unlocked (Research/Doctrine/Special Projects/Terrain stay exactly as set elsewhere, unless overridden below by a Research Year - this searches what's buildable right now, not a hypothetical research path) and ranks them by a chosen stat, optionally as a ratio of two stats (e.g. Organisation per Production Cost). Starts fresh each time - it does not look at whatever's currently placed in Composition.

Constraints (optional)

If no design can satisfy every constraint below, nothing is shown - loosen or remove one and try again.

Research Year (optional)

Require/Exclude Battalion(s)

Click once to require at least one of a type; click again to make it the dominant type (the largest single share of the division); click again to make it the ONLY type anywhere in the division; click again to exclude it entirely (e.g. make sure the division never includes Rangers); click again to clear.

Exclude an Entire Category

Removes every battalion type in that category from the search (e.g. exclude all Armored Combat Support), not just one type at a time. Click to exclude, click again to clear.

Require a Doctrine (optional)

Only matters when "Let AI choose Doctrine" below is checked - pins one Grand Doctrine and/or Subdoctrine while everything else about Doctrine still searches freely. Click to require, click again to clear.

Let AI Choose Other Modifiers

Unchecked, each one stays exactly as set on its own tab - checked, the AI searches that too instead of holding it fixed.

Tank Designer

Uncheck to see what's achievable with default/tier equipment alone instead of your own saved designs - nothing to "choose" here otherwise, since whichever battalions the search picks already use your saved template automatically whenever one applies.

Patch Impact Preview

Preview how a staged (not-yet-real) patch would change every division you've already saved, before deciding to actually apply it to this app's real data. Staging a patch is a manual step done with Claude in a session, the same way every real balance update (1.19.3, 1.19.4) has already been applied directly - this tab just previews the effect first.
